# HMM Flashcard Deck: Card #1, 人 (rén)

> **Deck conventions (set on Card #1, reused on all 500 cards)**
> - **Card size:** 63 × 88 mm (2.5 × 3.5 in, poker size), 3 mm bleed, 300 dpi. Artwork canvas: 819 × 1125 px plus bleed. Use `--ar 5:7` in Midjourney.
> - **Hanzi typeface:** a Kaiti (楷体) face, such as *LXGW WenKai* or *AR PL UKai*. Kaiti keeps the brush entry and exit shapes (the 丿 taper, the ㇏ flick) that the artwork traces. Sans-serif fonts flatten them.
> - **Latin typeface:** a humanist serif for titles, such as *Cormorant Garamond*. Use a clean sans for data lines, such as *Inter*.
> - **Tone colour band:** a thin strip down the back's left edge. T1 = silver, **T2 = green (rising)**, T3 = amber, T4 = red, neutral = grey.
> - **Production tip:** AI image models can't render Hanzi reliably. Generate the illustration **without text**, then place the real vector glyph over it in Figma, Affinity or Canva. The prompts below include `--no text`.

## --- FRONT OF CARD ---

### Target Hanzi

# 人

Centred and set in Kaiti. The glyph fills about 70% of the card height. Sumi ink black (#1B1B1F) at 85% opacity, in a **Multiply** blend mode so the watercolour shows through the strokes.

### Artwork Description & Overlay Blueprint

**Scene:** Aberdeen High Street climbs steeply **away from the viewer** toward a vanishing point at the top of the card. It has silver-grey granite buildings, wet cobblestones and a pale North Sea sky. **RuPaul** is seen from behind, three-quarter view, mid-stride in a wide runway power-march up the hill. The legs splay into a bold inverted V.

**Stroke-by-stroke overlay map:**

| Stroke | Order | Hanzi stroke | Traced by | Placement notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 丿 (piě, left-falling) | 1 | Starts at the top centre and sweeps down-left with a gentle curve, tapering to a point | RuPaul's **left leg**, from the hip down the thigh and calf to a pointed stiletto toe | The start of the stroke sits exactly at the **waist/hip join**. The leg bows slightly outward, like the stroke's curve. The pointed shoe is the stroke's tapered tip. |
| ㇏ (nà, right-falling) | 2 | Starts **slightly below** the top of 丿 and sweeps down-right, thickening into a flat, flared "foot" | RuPaul's **right leg**, from just below the hip down to a chunky platform heel | The thigh branches off *under* the hip point (Kaiti 人 is not a symmetric Λ). The **flared platform sole** is the characteristic ㇏ press-and-flick. |

**Composition rules:**
- The torso, arms and head sit **above** the glyph's apex and stay out of the character zone. Arms are raised in a victory pose, with a sequined cape billowing up-right. The cape doubles as a **rising-tone arrow** (↗).
- The legs are the darkest, highest-contrast shapes in the painting: deep plum or black tights against the pale granite. They read as the character at thumbnail size.
- The street's rising perspective lines (kerbs and tram-track grooves) converge upward to reinforce **Tone 2 = rising**.
- Keep the background soft, wet-in-wet and low-contrast. Save hard edges for the legs.
- Palette: granite silvers (#B8BCC4), slate blue (#5A6B7D) and wet-cobble charcoal. Accents are Tone-2 green on the cape lining (#3FA66B) and a sequin gold.

## --- BACK OF CARD ---

**人 | rén | Tone 2 | #1 | HSK 1**

**English Definition:** person; human being; people

**Mnemonic Title:** *RuPaul Struts Uphill*

### Full HMM Story

> **RuPaul** (**R-**) arrives in **Aberdeen** (**-EN**), the Granite City. RuPaul heads straight for the **High Street**, the steepest climb in town. This is the **rising-tone zone (Tone 2)**, and your voice climbs with it: *rén ↗*.
>
> Wind off the North Sea whips the cape upward, but RuPaul doesn't slow down. RuPaul plants one stiletto down-left and one platform heel down-right, legs spread in a wide runway march. From behind, the silhouette is just **two strong legs standing on the earth**, and that is what a **person** (人) is.
>
> RuPaul reaches the top, turns to the crowd and shouts: *"Every **person** on this street, you better work!"*

**Memory hooks:**
- Tone check: you can't march up that hill in a flat voice. It **rises**.
- Stroke order: the **left leg steps first** (丿). The right leg joins just below the hip (㇏).
- **Future prop:** when 人 appears as a component on later cards (亻 in 你, 他, 们, 休), bring on RuPaul's **stiletto** as the prop.
