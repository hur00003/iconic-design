# HMM Movies — Batch 02 (30 characters)

Props follow `props.md`.

### 1. 不 bù — no; not
**Brenda Felix** · Honolulu · Surf break (wipeout) · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* no-entry barrier (不)

**Brenda Felix** paddles out to the **surf break** off **Honolulu**, but a lifeguard slams a no-entry barrier across the wave: "**NO**! **Not** today!" Brenda wipes out into it.

*Words:* 不客气 · 对不起

### 2. 的 de — possessive particle ('s)
**Denzil Hurt** · Jacob's parents' place · Front hallway · Neutral  
*Props:* white paint (白) + soup ladle (勺)

In the quiet **front hallway at Jacob's parents' place**, **Denzil** dips a soup ladle into white paint and quietly labels every coat on the hooks: "Denzil**'s**. Denzil**'s**. Denzil**'s**."

*Words:* 我的 · 你的

### 3. 了 le — completed-action particle
**Laura** · Jacob's parents' place · Front hallway · Neutral  
*Props:* coat hook (乛) + fishing hook (亅)

**Laura** tiptoes into the **front hallway at Jacob's parents' place**, hangs a fishing hook on the coat hook and whispers: "**Done**." Job finished.

*Words:* 除了 · 为了

### 4. 你 nǐ — you
**Nhi Le** · Petřín Hill · Mirror maze · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* heels (亻) + hand mirror (尔)

Deep in the **Petřín mirror maze**, **Nhi Le** teeters in stilettos, holds up a hand mirror, and a hundred reflections point back: "**YOU**!"

*Words:* 你们 · 你好

### 5. 一 yī — one
**Weird Al** · Petřín Hill · Lookout tower top · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* oar (一)

At the **top of the Petřín lookout tower**, **Weird Al** lays **one** single oar perfectly flat along the railing. Just **one**.

*Words:* 一起 · 第一

### 6. 二 èr — two
**You** · Pearl District, Portland · Steps down to the Willamette · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* two oars (一 ×2)

**You** carry **two** oars, one stacked above the other, down the **steps from the Pearl District to the Willamette**. Practice starts in **two** minutes.

*Words:* 二

### 7. 五 wǔ — five
**Willy Wonka** · Honolulu · Hanauma Bay (snorkelling) · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* starfish (五)

Snorkelling in **Hanauma Bay**, **Willy Wonka** dips under and surfaces holding a starfish: "**Five** arms! **Five** golden tickets!"

*Words:* 五

### 8. 六 liù — six
**Laura** · Tokyo · Sumo ring (thrown down) · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* top hat (亠) + chopsticks spread apart (八)

In a **Tokyo sumo ring**, **Laura** in a top hat uses giant chopsticks to flip wrestlers down onto the clay: one, two… **six**!

*Words:* 六

### 9. 七 qī — seven
**Queen Elizabeth II (QE2)** · Petřín Hill · Lookout tower top · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* crowbar (七)

At the **top of the Petřín lookout tower**, **the Queen** pries open a locked hatch with a crowbar. Out march **seven** dwarfs.

*Words:* 七

### 10. 九 jiǔ — nine
**Julia Roberts** · Tokyo · Onsen hot bath · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* flexed arm (九)

Sinking into a **Tokyo onsen**, **Julia Roberts** flexes one arm out of the steam and counts rubber ducks bobbing past: … eight, **nine**!

*Words:* 九

### 11. 小 xiǎo — small; little
**Cher** · Cat café · Nap basket · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* fishing hook (亅) + two raindrops (丶丶)

In the **cat café nap basket**, **Cher** dangles a tiny fishing hook between two raindrops over a **little** kitten. Everything here is **small**.

*Words:* 小时 · 小心

### 12. 爱 ài — love
**You** · Sky3 · Elevator bank · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* claw (爫) + bedsheet (冖) + friendship bracelet (友)

At the **Sky3 elevator bank**, a claw-machine claw drops from the ceiling, lifts a bedsheet and reveals a friendship bracelet with **your** name on it. **You** fall in **love**.

*Words:* 爱好 · 可爱

### 13. 去 qù — go
**Queen Elizabeth II (QE2)** · Honolulu · Surf break (wipeout) · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* pot of soil (土) + traffic cone (厶)

At the **Honolulu surf break**, **the Queen** balances on a surfboard, sitting on a pot of soil with a traffic cone as a crown. A wave hits: "**GO**!" Wipeout.

*Words:* 去年 · 过去

### 14. 说 shuō — speak; say
**Shannon** · Waterfront Park, Portland · Esplanade walk · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* microphone (讠) + currency-exchange booth (兑)

Strolling the **Waterfront Park esplanade**, **Shannon** sets up a currency-exchange booth and **speaks** into a microphone: "**Say** the word, I'll swap your dollars!"

*Words:* 说话

### 15. 家 jiā — home; family
**Julia Roberts** · Ljubljana · Triple Bridge · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* pitched roof (宀) + pig (豕)

On **Ljubljana's Triple Bridge**, **Julia Roberts** builds a pitched roof over a pig. The pig moves in: **home**, sweet **home**, and the whole **family** comes too.

*Words:* 大家 · 国家

### 16. 水 shuǐ — water
**Shannon** · Norway · Cold plunge into the fjord · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* splashing fountain (水)

**Shannon** takes a cold plunge into a **Norwegian fjord** and comes up spouting **water** like a fountain.

*Words:* 水果 · 喝水

### 17. 书 shū — book
**Shannon** · Honolulu · Waikiki Beach · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* open book (书)

Stretched out flat on **Waikiki Beach**, **Shannon** reads an enormous open **book** as a sun shade.

*Words:* 图书馆

### 18. 天 tiān — sky; day
**Taylor Swift** · Vienna · Schönbrunn Palace gardens · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* oar (一) above oar + heels (大)

In the **Schönbrunn gardens**, **Taylor Swift** stands tall in stilettos, arms and oar out wide, and holds a second oar high overhead to point at the **sky**: "What a beautiful **day**!"

*Words:* 今天 · 明天

### 19. 年 nián — year
**Nhi Le** · Vienna · Prater Ferris wheel · Tone 2 (rising)  
*Props:* wall calendar (年)

Riding the **Prater Ferris wheel**, **Nhi Le** flips a wall calendar one page per carriage. By the top, a whole **year** has passed.

*Words:* 去年 · 今年

### 20. 月 yuè — moon; month
**Weird Al** · Utrecht · Bikes tumbling into the canal · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* joint of meat (月)

Under a full **moon** in **Utrecht**, **Weird Al** cycles along the canal with a joint of meat in the basket, hits a bollard and tumbles into the water. It happens every **month**.

*Words:* 月亮 · 一月

### 21. 下 xià — down; under; next
**Cher** · Ljubljana · Steps down to the river · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* flag hanging below an oar (下)

On the **steps down to the river in Ljubljana**, **Cher** holds an oar level and lowers a flag **DOWN** underneath it, step by step.

*Words:* 下午 · 下雨

### 22. 回 huí — return; go back
**Hugh Jackman** · Norway · Pulpit Rock climb · Tone 2 (rising)  
*Props:* crate (囗) around wind-up teeth (口)

Halfway up **Pulpit Rock**, **Hugh Jackman** realises the chattering wind-up teeth were left in a crate at the bottom and has to **go back** down to fetch them.

*Words:* 回答 · 回家

### 23. 会 huì — can; be able to; meeting
**Hugh Jackman** · Norway · Seven Sisters waterfall · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* heels (人) + cloud (云)

At the **Seven Sisters waterfall**, **Hugh Jackman** steps off the cliff in stilettos onto a cloud and rides it down the falls: "I **can** do this!"

*Words:* 会议 · 机会

### 24. 叫 jiào — be called; call; shout
**Julia Roberts** · Cat café · Cat pouncing off a shelf · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* wind-up teeth (口) + tangled rope (丩)

A cat pounces off a **cat café** shelf, tangling **Julia Roberts** in rope. Wind-up teeth **shout** for help: "**Call** the manager!"

*Words:* 叫

### 25. 听 tīng — listen; hear
**Taylor Swift** · Burger King · Counter · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* wind-up teeth (口) + hatchet (斤)

At the **Burger King counter**, wind-up teeth chatter next to a hatchet. **Taylor Swift** leans in and **listens** very carefully.

*Words:* 听说 · 听见

### 26. 想 xiǎng — want; think; miss
**Cher** · Willamette River (dragon boat) · Hull dipping through the stroke · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* tree (木) + giant eyeball (目) + beating heart (心)

In the dragon boat on the **Willamette**, the hull dips with each stroke. **Cher** stares at a tree with a giant eyeball in it and **thinks**, heart beating: "I **want** to go home."

*Words:* 想 · 理想

### 27. 写 xiě — write
**Cher** · Aberdeen Beach · Sand dunes · Tone 3 (dipping)

In the **Aberdeen Beach dunes**, **Cher** ducks under a bedsheet shelter and **writes** a card to tuck inside a gift box.

*Words:* 写 · 写字

### 28. 做 zuò — do; make
**Zac Efron** · Waterfront Park, Portland · Rolling down the grassy Bowl · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* heels (亻) + first-aid cross (十) + wind-up teeth (口) + whip (攵)

**Zac Efron** rolls down the grassy **Bowl** at **Waterfront Park** in stilettos, cracking a whip at wind-up teeth wearing a first-aid cross: "Get to work! **Do** something! **Make** something!"

### 29. 坐 zuò — sit; take (transport)
**Zac Efron** · Waterfront Park, Portland · Rolling down the grassy Bowl · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* two pairs of heels (人人) + pot of soil (土)

At the bottom of the grassy **Bowl**, two pairs of stilettos **sit** side by side on a pot of soil. **Zac Efron** rolls down and **sits** between them.

*Words:* 坐车

### 30. 住 zhù — live; reside; stay
**Judge Judy** · Honolulu · Surf break (wipeout) · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* heels (亻) + candle lamp (主)

**Judge Judy** wipes out at the **Honolulu surf break**, drags stilettos up the beach and lights a candle lamp in a surf shack: "I **live** here now."
