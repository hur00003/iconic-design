# HMM Movies — Batch 01 (30 HSK 1 characters)

Props follow `props.md`.

### 1. 人 rén — person; people
**RuPaul** · Aberdeen · High street · Tone 2 (rising)  
*Props:* heels (人)

**RuPaul** power-marches up the steep **High Street** in **Aberdeen**, legs splayed wide in plum stilettos. From behind it's just two legs planted on the ground: a **person**.

*Words:* 别人 · 客人

### 2. 大 dà — big; large
**Denzil Hurt** · Praha · Vltava riverbank · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* oar (一) + heels (人)

On the **Vltava riverbank** in **Praha**, **Denzil** struts down the steps in plum stilettos, holding an oar out level with both arms: "The fish was THIS **big**!" Then slips. SPLASH.

*Words:* 大家 · 大学

### 3. 我 wǒ — I; me
**Willy Wonka** · Costco · Checkout line · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* foam hand (手) + halberd (戈)

Stuck in the **Costco checkout line**, **Willy Wonka** waves a giant foam hand and swings a halberd over the crowd: "**ME**! It's **MY** turn!"

*Words:* 我们

### 4. 好 hǎo — good; well
**Hugh Jackman** · Nashville townhouse · Upstairs bedroom · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* veil (女) + baby car seat (子)

In the **upstairs bedroom** of the **Nashville townhouse**, **Hugh Jackman** finds a wedding veil draped over a baby's car seat, tucks the baby in and gives a thumbs up: "All **good**."

*Words:* 好吃 · 爱好

### 5. 是 shì — be; is; are; yes
**Shannon** · Karlštejn Castle · Dungeon · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* sun lamp (日) + hiking boot (𤴓)

Deep in the **Karlštejn Castle dungeon**, **Shannon** switches on a sun lamp. Its beam lands on a single hiking boot: "Yes! That **is** the one!"

*Words:* 还是 · 总是

### 6. 有 yǒu — have; there is
**Weird Al** · Hluboká Castle · Tower · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* oven mitt (ナ) + joint of meat (月)

**Weird Al** bursts out at the top of **Hluboká Castle's tower**, holding a sizzling joint of meat in an oven mitt: "I **HAVE** it!"

*Words:* 没有 · 有名

### 7. 在 zài — at; in; be located
**Zac Efron** · Sky3 · Elevator bank · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* oven mitt (ナ) + pot of soil (土)

At the **Sky3 elevator bank**, **Zac Efron** plants a pot of soil on the floor with an oven mitt and stands on the spot: "I'm right **here**. This is where I **am**."

*Words:* 现在 · 正在

### 8. 这 zhè — this
**Judge Judy** · Jacob's parents' place · Backyard · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* Segway (辶) + scroll (文)

**Judge Judy** rides a Segway across the **backyard at Jacob's parents' place**, slams a scroll onto the patio table and points: "**THIS** one!"

*Words:* 这里 · 这样

### 9. 他 tā — he; him
**Taylor Swift** · Praha · Charles Bridge · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* heels (亻) + coiled snake (也)

On **Charles Bridge** in **Praha**, **Taylor Swift** spots a pair of men's-size stilettos with a snake coiled inside: "Whose are these? **HIS**!"

*Words:* 其他 · 他们

### 10. 她 tā — she; her
**Taylor Swift** · Praha · Charles Bridge · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* veil (女) + coiled snake (也)

On **Charles Bridge**, **Taylor Swift** finds a wedding veil with a snake coiled inside: "Whose is this? **HERS**!"

*Words:* 她们

### 11. 中 zhōng — middle; China
**Judge Judy** · St. Vitus Cathedral · Main nave · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* wind-up teeth (口) + flagpole (丨)

In the **main nave of St. Vitus Cathedral**, **Judge Judy** drives a flagpole straight through the **middle** of a set of chattering wind-up teeth, dead centre of the aisle.

*Words:* 中国 · 中午

### 12. 上 shàng — up; on; previous
**Shannon** · Jacob's current apartment · Bedroom · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* flag raised on a pole (上)

In the **bedroom of Jacob's apartment**, **Shannon** lays an oar on the floor, stands a flagpole on it and runs a flag **UP** the pole.

*Words:* 上午 · 晚上

### 13. 来 lái — come
**Laura** · Sky3 · Gym / fitness room · Tone 2 (rising)  
*Props:* wheat sheaf (来)

In the **Sky3 gym**, **Laura** waves a huge wheat sheaf like a pom-pom at everyone on the treadmills: "**Come** on! **Come** here!"

*Words:* 后来 · 起来

### 14. 开 kāi — open; drive; start
**Kevin (Home Alone)** · Sky3 · Rooftop kitchen · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* oar (一) + garden gloves (廾)

Locked out of the **Sky3 rooftop kitchen**, **Kevin** pulls on garden gloves and levers the door **OPEN** with an oar.

*Words:* 开始 · 离开

### 15. 看 kàn — look; watch; read
**Kevin (Home Alone)** · Anděl · Tram stop · Tone 4 (falling)  
*Props:* foam hand (手) + giant eyeball (目)

At the **Anděl tram stop**, **Kevin** holds a foam hand over a giant eyeball to shade it, **looking** down the tracks for the tram.

*Words:* 看见

### 16. 吃 chī — eat
**Channing Tatum** · Karlštejn Castle · Gatehouse · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* wind-up teeth (口) + beggar's tin cup (乞)

At the **Karlštejn gatehouse**, **Channing Tatum** shakes a beggar's tin cup. Chattering wind-up teeth leap in and **eat** every coin.

*Words:* 好吃

### 17. 喝 hē — drink
**Hugh Jackman** · Jacob's parents' place · Living room · Tone 1 (flat)  
*Props:* wind-up teeth (口) + sun lamp (日) + sleeping bag (匃)

In the **living room at Jacob's parents' place**, **Hugh Jackman** lies in a sleeping bag under a sun lamp, parched, while wind-up teeth **drink** the lemonade.

*Props:* coat hook (乛) + mannequin head (头)

**Nhi Le** · Old Town Square · Astronomical Clock · Tone 2 (rising)

### 20. 很 hěn — very
**Hugh Jackman** · Aberdeen · Train station · Tone 3 (dipping)  
*Props:* treadmill (彳) + stubborn mule (艮)

At **Aberdeen train station**, **Hugh Jackman** is on a treadmill, trying to drag a stubborn mule onto the train. The mule is **VERY** stubborn.

*Words:* 很多

**Shannon** · Karlštejn Castle · Great Tower · Tone 2 (rising)
