## --- FRONT OF CARD ---

### Target Hanzi

# 大

Deck-standard layout: a large, centred Kaiti glyph spanning about 65% of the card height. Sumi ink black (#1B1B1F) at 60% opacity in Multiply blend mode. The 一 lines up with Denzil's outstretched arms and oar. The 丿 and ㇏ run alongside the body and legs.

### Artwork Description & Overlay Blueprint

**Scene:** Prague's **Vltava riverbank**: a stone embankment with wide steps running **down** to the water, swans, moored wooden rowing boats, and soft misty river light. **Denzil** faces the viewer, halfway down the steps, mid-stomp. Arms are stretched straight out sideways in the classic fisherman's *"it was THIS big!"* brag, with a long wooden oar held level across both hands. On Denzil's feet are **RuPaul's stiletto and platform heel from Card #1**, the prop for the 人 component.

**Stroke-by-stroke overlay map (stroke order 一 → 丿 → ㇏):**

| Stroke | Order | Hanzi stroke | Traced by | Placement notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 一 (héng, horizontal) | 1 | Level line across the upper third | Denzil's **outstretched arms + the oar** | Perfectly horizontal and wider than the body. This is the key difference from 人, where the arms are raised. |
| 丿 (piě, left-falling) | 2 | Starts **above** the 一, passes through it, sweeps down-left | **Head → spine → left leg**, one continuous gently curving line ending in the pointed stiletto | The head pokes above the oar, as the 丿 starts above the 一. |
| ㇏ (nà, right-falling) | 3 | Starts where 丿 crosses 一 and sweeps down-right to a flared foot | **Right leg**, branching from just under the arm line, ending in the flared platform heel | Long legs and a short torso make the geometry work. |

**Composition rules:**
- Arms and oar are the strongest horizontal in the image. Keep every other horizon (railings, the waterline) soft and low-contrast.
- **Tone 4 = falling:** Denzil is stepping **down** the embankment toward the water. Spray from a splash below and a swan diving downward reinforce the drop (↘).
- Keep the centre pale and uncluttered (the glyph sits there). Put detail at the edges: boats, swans, a distant misty Prague skyline. Leave Charles Bridge out, because it belongs to Tone 1's area.
- Palette: river blue-greys (#6F8A99), warm sandstone embankment (#C9B79C), dark oar and legs for contrast. Accents: **Tone-4 red** (#C8453A) scarf or sash, and the plum heels from Card #1.

## --- BACK OF CARD ---

**大 | dà | Tone 4 | #2 | HSK 1**

**English Definition:** big; large; great; (of age) older

**Mnemonic Title:** *Denzil's Big Fish Tale*

### Full HMM Story

> **Denzil** (**D-**) is in **Praha** (**-A**), heading **down** the stone steps to the **Vltava riverbank**, the **falling-tone zone (Tone 4)**. Say it like a sharp drop: *dà ↘!*
>
> Denzil has borrowed **RuPaul's heels** from Card #1 (the 人 prop). Now Denzil is telling the swans about a fish: arms flung out wide, oar held level, *"It was THIS **BIG**!"* That level oar is the extra stroke (一) that turns a person (人) into **big** (大).
>
> Mid-brag, one stiletto slips on the wet stone, and Denzil **drops** into the river. **SPLASH!** A *big* one.

**Memory hooks:**
- **人 + 一 = 大:** a person stretching their arms wide to show how big something is.
- **Contrast with Card #1:** RuPaul's arms go **up** (人, person). Denzil's arms go **out** (大, big).
- **Stroke order:** arms and oar first (一), then head-to-left-foot (丿), then the right leg (㇏).
- **Future prop:** when 大 appears inside later characters (太, 天, 夫, 美), bring on Denzil's **oar**.
